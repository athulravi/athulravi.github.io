The Human Behind the Work

Hey! I'm an Instructional Designer, learning enthusiast, and someone who genuinely enjoys figuring out how people learn best.

Over the last 7 years, I've worked across industries, turning complex ideas into engaging learning experiences. From e-learning and interactive modules to technical training, I enjoy exploring the intersection of learning, design, technology, and creativity.

I believe good learning design isn't just about delivering information; it's about making people curious enough to want to learn more.
