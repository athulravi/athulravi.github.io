About the data and the method

Source

ILEDA dataset: anonymised xAPI statements from Moodle and LAMS learning environments at four universities (Zenodo, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.10114515), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. All credit for data collection and publication goes to the dataset's authors. This dashboard shows only aggregated results (no learner-level records) and is shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence. Analysis and dashboard: Athul Nath P.

How the numbers are built

  • Weeks are counted from each learner's own first activity in a course, so courses with different calendars are comparable. "Active" means at least one logged event that week.
  • Content coverage is the share of a course's learners with at least one logged event of that content type. Hatched cells mean the LMS logged nothing of that type for the course.
  • Deadline proxy: the dataset has no due dates, so each assignment's deadline is approximated by the 90th-percentile submission time. Only assignments with enough submissions are used (40+, or 150+ in Human Factors, which has many small items).
  • Study times use each university's local time zone. Timestamps in the BMU courses may be offset, which makes their time-of-day unreliable, so BMU is left out of the pooled view.
  • Learner types come from a transparent rule set: Early leaver (active span of 3 weeks or less, or under half the course median); Bursty (half or more of all events in their two busiest weeks); Steady (active in 75% or more of weeks); otherwise On-and-off. Scores are mean scaled scores of scored learners.

What this cannot tell you

  • Viewing is not learning: activity shows behaviour, not understanding.
  • No due dates and no final grades are in the data, so deadlines are a proxy and outcomes are limited to logged scores.
  • Logging differs by course (for example BMU only logs quizzes and assessments), so cross-course comparisons of content use are partly comparisons of instrumentation.
  • Scores in the UL Web applications course are unreliable, so its scores are not shown, and only 12 SU Human Computer Interaction learners have a score.
  • Links between learner type and score are associations. Only 15 learners are Early leavers with a score.
